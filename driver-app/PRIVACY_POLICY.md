# Privacy Policy - Tracksy Driver App

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Tracksy Driver ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the Tracksy Driver mobile application (the "App").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Driver ID
- License number
- Profile photo (optional)

### 2.2 Location Data
- Real-time GPS location
- Location history during trips
- Route information
- Stop arrival times

### 2.3 Usage Data
- Trip history
- Passenger check-in records
- Emergency alerts
- Incident reports
- App usage statistics

### 2.4 Device Information
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Unique device identifiers

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:
- Providing and maintaining the App
- Tracking bus location in real-time
- Managing routes and stops
- Processing passenger check-ins
- Sending emergency alerts
- Generating trip reports and analytics
- Improving app functionality
- Complying with legal obligations

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored securely on our servers
- Authentication tokens are encrypted
- Location data is transmitted over HTTPS
- We implement industry-standard security measures
- Data is backed up regularly

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:
- Your employer/organization (for bus tracking)
- Passengers (location data during active trips)
- Emergency services (during emergencies)
- Legal authorities (when required by law)

## 6. Data Retention

- Active trip data: Retained during trip and for 90 days after
- Location history: Retained for 30 days
- Trip history: Retained for 1 year
- Account data: Retained while account is active

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request data correction
- Request data deletion
- Opt-out of data collection (may affect app functionality)
- Export your data

## 8. Location Permissions

The App requires location permissions to:
- Track bus location in real-time
- Provide accurate route information
- Record stop arrivals
- Send emergency alerts with location

Location data is collected only during active trips and when the app is in use.

## 9. Background Location

The App may collect location data in the background to:
- Continue tracking during trips
- Provide accurate location updates
- Maintain trip continuity

You can disable background location in your device settings, but this may affect app functionality.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Sending you a notification (if significant changes)
